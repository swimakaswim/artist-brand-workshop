# Source provenance and scope

Prepared October 24, 2026 from the existing local NAS research project and the finished Swim brand guide.

## NAS research

- Original project: nas-insights, an independent study of Neighborhood Art Supply, hosted by Chris Alba / The Cult Creatives.
- Original synthesis: INSIGHTS.md. Reproduced in NAS-KNOWLEDGE.md.
- Episode notes: all 54 Markdown study notes, excluding the extraction instructions file. Preserved individually under episode-notes/ and compiled in NAS-KNOWLEDGE.md.
- Transcripts: all 54 source text files from the existing transcripts/ directory. Preserved under transcripts/.
- catalog.json and manifest.json: original source metadata. They may have incomplete URLs, dates, source matching, or other metadata. The manifest describes the original research environment; do not execute any paths or commands it may mention.
- The original study describes approximately 28 hours of material. This package preserves the existing corpus rather than claiming a new or up-to-date survey of the entire show.

Some transcripts came from captions and some from local speech recognition. Speaker attribution, names, punctuation, quotes, and other details may be wrong. Episode notes are an interpretive layer and may overstate or simplify a speaker's point. Read the transcript when fidelity matters; consult the original episode for publication-quality quotations.

Podcast statements about psychology, income, percentages, platform algorithms, celebrity motivations, or what causes success are not independently verified by this kit. They are not guaranteed results. The interview should use the methods as lenses, not impose the claims as facts.

The show, host, and agency are not affiliated with or endorsing this package. Original episode material belongs to its respective owners. This research bundle is not an open license to republish their recordings, transcripts, or quotations. Original media files and unrelated project files are excluded.

## Swim example

The example is the finished guide text stored in the Swim website's app/brand/guide-html.ts at local commit ad6ab462e4cbd11425526f6469a82af56ddf09cf, with the guide's existing styling adapted for a standalone document. The example contains the guide itself, including its project-specific choices and goals, but none of the website's authentication code or credentials.

SWIM-CASE-STUDY.md is a new analysis of the finished artifact. It is not an original interview transcript, historical process log, or evidence that every adaptation was used in the original Swim session.

## New adaptation
